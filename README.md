# AIMS Study Fair 2026 — English Challenge

Interactive English quiz built for stall engagement at the **AIMS Study Fair 2026**, held at **The Peninsula**.

- **Developer:** Md Mahmudul Hoque Khan
- **Built with:** OpenAI Codex


## About

Visitors to the AIMS stall scan the code, enter their name, and take a 75-second English challenge — 7 regular questions plus 3 special challenge questions, drawn at random from a large pool.

High scores are shown on a live score dashboard and can be redeemed for a surprise at the stall counter, driving foot traffic and conversation at the fair.

## Features

- 10 random questions per attempt, 75-second countdown
- Regular + special challenge question pools
- Answer review with correct/incorrect breakdown
- Score dashboard (localStorage, per-device)
- Mobile-friendly, single-file deployment
